Action Plan Year 1

Education and Outreach

  1. Issue quarterly informational emails regarding seasonal fire risks and relevant updates of fire protection recommendations and regulations.
  2. Conduct a resident outreach to survey interest and to recruit additional households.
  3. Expand neighborhood participation in Firewise program from the current 24 households to at least 34 households (17%) by year end 2027.
  4. Circulate Lafayette City Wildfire Guide.
  5. Circulate updated list of preferred local fire protection vendors for home hardening and fuel reduction.
  6. Share examples of residents completing fire protection effort.
  7. Organize a neighborhood meeting with Lafayette Police Department representative to discuss emergency evacuation.

Action Plan Year 1

Home Hardening

  1. Encourage residents to address roof hardening with the following actions:
    1. Install 1/8 inch metal mesh screening to roof vents and eaves.
    2. Replace and repair any loose or missing shingles or roof tiles to prevent ember penetration.
    3. Install gutter covers.
  2. Check chimneys for proper spark, ember protection and apply proper screening as necessary.
  3. Screen or box-in areas below patios and decks with fine gauge mesh to prevent debris and combustible materials from accumulating.
  4. Share examples of home hardening completed in neighborhood and recommended home hardening vendors.

Action Plan Year 1

Defensible Space / Fuel Reduction

  1. Removal of dead trees and brush.
  2. Removal of highly flammable plants and trees (i.e. Eucalyptus).
  3. Work on creating and maintaining 0-5ft noncombustible and ember resistant zone around structures.
  4. Replace wood mulch products with non-combustible alternatives.
  5. Remove anything stored underneath decks and porches.

Action Plan Year 1

Evacuation Planning and Wildfire Preparedness

  1. Encourage residents to sign up for the Contra Costa County Community Warning System at www.cwsalerts.com.
  2. Schedule a neighborhood meeting (either via Zoom or in person) with the Lafayette City Police Department to discuss emergency evacuation.
  3. Participate in any City/County/Fire Districts evacuation drills.
