Las Trampas Firewise Neighborhood

A neighbor-led wildfire community for Evacuation Area 016. Firewise USA approved the neighborhood’s application on September 25, 2026. The community covers about 193 homes and is listed on the Firewise plan as Las Trampas LAF-016.

Community Wildfire Action Plan

Las Trampas LAF-016 · 2027–2029 · Action Plan Year 1

Participation target

From 24 households to at least 34 (17%) by year end 2027.

That target is Year 1 education and outreach goal 3. See it in the plan.

Education and Outreach

Quarterly emails on seasonal fire risks, outreach to more households, the city wildfire guide, and a meeting with Lafayette police.

Home Hardening

Roof vents and eaves, loose shingles, gutter covers, chimney screening, and the space under patios and decks.

Defensible Space / Fuel Reduction

Dead trees and brush, highly flammable plants, the 0-5ft zone, mulch, and what is stored under decks and porches.

Evacuation Planning and Wildfire Preparedness

Sign up for the Contra Costa Community Warning System, and plan evacuation with the Lafayette police.

Local programs

City, fire district, and regional programs that apply to this neighborhood.

Chipping days

Con Fire’s Measure X Community Chipping Days are available to Firewise communities or neighborhoods with at least 10 participating homes. Recognized Firewise communities can apply through Con Fire’s Wildfire Mitigation Program.

Measure X Wildfire Mitigation Program

Insurance and Firewise

Under California’s Safer from Wildfires rules, insurers must offer discounts for qualifying mitigation, and living in a recognized Firewise USA community is one of the qualifying actions.

CA Department of Insurance: Safer from Wildfires
