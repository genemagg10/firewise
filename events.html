For our neighborhood

Free chipping days for our street group

Con Fire’s Measure X Community Chipping Days are available to Firewise communities or neighborhoods with at least 10 participating homes. We can apply through Con Fire’s Wildfire Mitigation Program application form.

Measure X Wildfire Mitigation Program

Firewise recognition can lower insurance costs

Under California’s Safer from Wildfires rules, insurers must offer discounts for qualifying mitigation, and living in a recognized Firewise USA community is one of the qualifying actions.

CA Department of Insurance: Safer from Wildfires

City of Lafayette: Wildfire Preparedness and Firewise

Know your zone and get alerts

  • Our neighborhood is Evacuation Area 016. Confirm your exact zone at Genasys Protect.
  • Register every phone with the Contra Costa Community Warning System (CWS).
  • Lafayette PD Nixle alerts: text 94549 to 888777.
  • Lafayette’s AM 1670 emergency radio has been offline since Aug 2026, according to the City’s notice, with no restore date. Don’t rely on it; make sure you’re signed up for CWS and Nixle.

In an emergency, call 911 and follow official orders. Leave early if you feel unsafe.
